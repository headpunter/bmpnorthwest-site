What we do

  • Weekly and post-storm inspections

    Every site walked weekly and within 24 hours of a qualifying storm, with the signed inspection form delivered the same day.

  • Permit compliance management

    Tracking inspection cadence, stabilization deadlines, and the 2026 permit changes so nothing lapses between visits.

  • DMR and permit reporting

    Monthly Discharge Monitoring Reports filed on time through Ecology's portal, including the signatory delegation paperwork most sites never finish.

  • Turbidity and pH sampling

    Meter-based sampling at discharge points during rain events, with calibration logged and results recorded on the inspection form.

  • BMP checks and contractor direction

    Clear written instructions on what to fix before the next visit, so the crew knows exactly what is expected and the paper trail shows it.

About Max Day

Max grew up around construction sites and has worked in the industry for 15 years. He has inspected everything from small commercial pads to a multi-phase, multi-building mixed-use development with five stormwater vaults and monitored discharges during rain events.
