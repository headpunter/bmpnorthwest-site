# BMP Northwest Consulting — design system

## Product
One-page marketing site for a one-person construction stormwater inspection business in Washington State (owner is a Washington CESCL, based in Centralia). Audience: general contractors, site superintendents, developers and owners who need permit-required stormwater inspections, turbidity/pH sampling and monthly Discharge Monitoring Reports. Job to be done: convince a busy contractor in a few seconds that this is handled, then get them to reveal the phone/email and call.

## Content rules (hard)
- Keep all existing copy verbatim. No new claims, stats, testimonials, client names, project names or city names.
- No phone number or email in the page. Contact is revealed only by the "Show phone and email" button (Cloudflare Turnstile). Keep that button in the hero and the footer.
- No SWPPP-writing or binder/logbook services. No CESCL certificate number.
- Keep the "Where we work" county map: 19 Western Washington counties, each county a hoverable shape that shows its name.
- Tone: plain, confident, field-practical. Not corporate, not techy.

## Brand
- Colors: ink #16222B, slate #243441, rain #E7ECEF, paper #F7F8F9, safety orange #E5641F, orange-ink #B84B12, mute #5B6B77, line #C9D2D8.
- Font: Archivo (variable width). Wide 700 for headings, regular for body.
- Logo: orange rounded square with four stacked white horizontal strokes (sediment layers), wordmark "BMP Northwest Consulting".
- Visual vocabulary from the field: rain, silt fence, sediment layers, survey/plan-sheet linework, safety orange. Avoid: SaaS card grids, gradients, glassmorphism, stock-photo handshakes, cream-and-terracotta.

## Layout
Single column page, max 1080px, left-aligned text. Must work at 375px phone width.
